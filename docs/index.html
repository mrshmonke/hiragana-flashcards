Voice

Voice runs offline on this computer through the free voice helper (Whisper to hear you, Open JTalk and Piper to speak). Nothing you say is sent anywhere except the text you send to Claude.

Free and offline. Downloads about 1 GB once (speech recognition, Japanese and English voices). Takes a few minutes.

Bigger models hear Japanese better but take longer, and each downloads once (tiny 75 MB, base 150 MB, small 500 MB, medium 1.5 GB, large turbo 1.6 GB) the first time you use it.

Natural Japanese voices (VOICEVOX)
Much clearer, human-sounding Japanese than the basic voice. Free and offline, made by a Japanese open-source project (nothing from Google). Downloads about 145 MB once. VOICEVOX and each voice have terms of use: free for personal study; if you ever publish audio made with it, credit it as "VOICEVOX:" plus the voice's name.
